Mock experience guide

Money, handled with care and context.

Payments is an interactive CRM prototype for collection work that keeps financial state, relationship history, and the next considerate action visible together.

Money states are explicit

Pending, paid, settled, refunded, and disputed activity are never collapsed into a vague success state.

Relationships come first

Payments connect to people, organizations, campaigns, receipts, and commitments instead of an isolated ledger.

Sensitive actions show evidence

Recovery, refund, dispute, and reconciliation flows state their outcome and add a visible audit cue.

Key workflows

What the mock lets you explore.

Collect with intent

Start a donation page, invoice, payment link, or manual payment flow with relationship context and a plain-language audit statement.

Try donor checkout

Configure cadence, amount, designation, fee coverage, anonymity, and card or bank-debit behavior. Bank debit remains pending until settlement.

Resolve a payment event

Review transaction evidence, focus a relationship, and walk through refund and dispute actions as accountable, visible workflows.

Protect donor control

Recurring commitments support direct pause and cancellation actions without hiding those choices behind retention patterns.

Prototype boundary

This is a stateful front-end mock. It does not collect credentials, charge a payment method, call a gateway, create accounting entries, send receipts, or enforce backend permissions.
