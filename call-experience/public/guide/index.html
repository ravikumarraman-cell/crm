Mock experience guide

Conversations with their context intact.

Calls is an interactive CRM prototype for planning, completing, and following through on conversations without separating the call from the relationship.

Context before contact

Every call begins with the person, their commitments, and the next useful question.

One canonical record

Scheduled, completed, and manually logged calls converge in a searchable activity history.

Visible follow-through

Wrap-up, tasks, and relationship timelines make the next commitment accountable.

Key workflows

What the mock lets you explore.

Run a call

Choose a priority contact, start an outbound or simulated inbound call, use mute, hold, or warm transfer, then capture the outcome and a next step in wrap-up.

Find the history

Use Call Finder or the History filters to locate follow-ups, meetings, no answers, voicemails, softphone activity, and uncontacted prospects.

Keep the relationship whole

Open the relationship record to review timeline context, edit a note, add a task, or return to a prepared call or schedule action.

Operate safely

Administration demonstrates policy choices for recording, transcript access, retention, routing, provider health, and recovery.

Prototype boundary

This is a stateful front-end mock. It uses seeded data and browser-local persistence to demonstrate interaction design. It does not connect to a dialer, CRM, transcription provider, identity system, or external service.
