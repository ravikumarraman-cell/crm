Key workflows

What the mock lets you explore.

Build with intent

Configure a relationship outcome, CRM destination, channel, approved questions, routing policy, consent, and a respondent preview across five stages.

Manage lifecycle

Move form records through Draft, Review, Scheduled, Live, Paused, Retired, and Archived states while inspecting an illustrative version comparison.

Recover submissions

Assign Priya to Maya, confirm Jordan's identity, or replay Sofia's delivery while preserving a visible evidence trail and idempotency context.

Operate with governance

Explore routing policy, automation sequence, governed assets, consent and data-handling settings, experiment guardrails, and a governance ledger.

Prototype boundary

This is a browser-state mock. It demonstrates operational UX but does not provide authentication, durable immutable storage, CRM integration, server-side matching, real workflow execution, or production security controls.
